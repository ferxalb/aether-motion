# AETHER Motion architecture

Source layout and resource limits of the native renderer. Compositions with `data-hf-split-wrap` are parsed twice (`parse::parse_file_with`): the renderer measures the first parse's unwrapped rows and the second parse builds the wrapped ones, so line breaks come from the same fonts and layout that draw them. Contributor rules (CPU budget, benchmarking, tests) live in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md); measurements live in [BENCHMARK.md](../BENCHMARK.md).

## Source layout

- `src/model.rs`: parsed composition, styles, and time-sampled motion tracks.
- `src/parse/`: HTML/CSS parser; unsupported features fail with explicit errors. `mod.rs` is the `parse_file` facade; `css/` (stylesheet, selectors, cascade, variables), `values/` (lengths, colors, backgrounds, clip paths and masks, filters, transforms, shadows), `style/` (layout, text, and visual property groups), `animation/` (CSS animation, `data-hf-keyframes`, and `data-hf-camera` into motion tracks, including animated filter amounts), `rich_text.rs` (inline `<span>`/`<strong>`/`<b>`/`<em>`/`<i>`/`<code>` runs folded into their element's text as `model::TextSpan`s), `split/` (`data-hf-split` kinetic text: `mod.rs` builds one flex row of pieces, or a column of line rows for text with `<br>`, wrapping, line motion, or line masks, with optional per-word clip boxes; letters record the next letter in `LayoutHints::kern_after`; `motion.rs` holds the start values, stagger orders, and eased entrance tracks of pieces and lines; `SplitPlan` carries the measured line breaks of `data-hf-split-wrap` from the first parse to the second), `effects.rs` (`data-hf-bloom` and per-element `data-hf-motion-blur` into `model::ElementEffects`, the keyframed bloom intensity, and the checks that reject per-element blur with the root shutter, nested, or on the root), `captions.rs` (`data-hf-captions` rows of per-word highlight pieces built from a transcript), `transition.rs` (`data-hf-transition` scene transitions, compiled into pose, filter, and pixel-effect tracks), `variables/` (`data-composition-variables` declarations and value coercion in `declare.rs`, and the `data-var-text`, `data-var-src`, and `data-var-if` bindings in `bind.rs`, applied to the DOM before the stylesheet is read; the `--id` custom properties are pinned on the composition root through `Stylesheet::pinned`), `tree.rs` (DOM walk), `timeline.rs` (`data-start`), `audio.rs` (`<audio>` attributes into `AudioClip`s, kept outside the element tree), `audio_effects.rs` (`data-hf-volume-ramp`, `data-hf-eq`, and `data-hf-duck` for `<audio>` and video sound, and the trigger id resolution), `html.rs` (node helpers).
- `src/render/`: software renderer. `mod.rs` holds the public `Renderer` and frame loop; `draw.rs` walks the scene; `layout/` sizes and places flex items; `paint/` holds the `Surface` pixel buffer and its primitives (fills, gradients and soft shadows backed by a memory-bounded tile cache in `tile_cache.rs`, borders, outer and inset box shadows, backdrop blur, element effects in `effects/` (the offscreen layer for `filter` blur, color functions in `color.rs` (also the root grade), `drop-shadow`, `mix-blend-mode` compositing in `blend.rs`, the tint, channel split, and glitch pixel effects of `model::FxMotion` in `fx.rs`, the `data-hf-bloom` halo in `bloom.rs` (bright pass box-averaged into a 1x, 2x, or 4x reduced grid, blurred there, bilinearly upsampled, tinted, and added with an ordered dither after the element is composited; its planes live in a `BloomScratch` reused across frames), and the continuous blur of animated filters in `exact_blur.rs`; effect layers other than a static blur share the frame's tile caches, frozen while both drawing passes run), images with cached row spans, `clip-path` and `mask-image` layers in `masked.rs` (shape coverage from the exact-area polygon rasterizer in `coverage.rs`, mask alpha from `gradient.rs`), rotation, the vignette/grain `finish` pass, `float_ops.rs`, exact call-free `round`/`hypot` replacements for per-pixel loops, and `kernels.rs`, branch-free per-row kernels (two-stop gradient rows, radial gradient parameters, cached gradient texels, shadow masks) compiled for the baseline target and for AVX2 and chosen at run time, bit-identical by construction and tested against the scalar loops they replaced; `finish.rs` dispatches its row loops the same way); `text/` handles fonts (`fonts.rs`: requests by family list, weight, and italic flag, keeping face bytes for outlines and marking italic requests that resolved to an upright face), pair kerning for `font-kerning: normal` (`kerning.rs`: GPOS pair adjustment lookups of the `kern` feature or the legacy `kern` table, memoized per pair, plus `Renderer::text_kerning`, the per-character adjustments shared by layout and painting), the synthetic oblique (`oblique.rs`: a 14 degree shear of glyph coverage around the baseline; strokes shear their outlines), inherited properties and per-run styles of inline rich text (`props.rs`: `RunStyle`, `style_index`), glyph strokes from font outlines (`stroke.rs`, `ttf-parser` outlines stroked by `tiny-skia`), line wrapping (`wrap.rs`), block layout (`block.rs`), glyph painting with fixed-size coverage masks and subpixel resampling (`mask.rs`) for continuous translation/zoom, and `text-shadow` (`shadow.rs`); `shutter.rs` averages sub-frame samples for `data-motion-blur`; `layer_blur.rs` averages one subtree for `data-hf-motion-blur` (it plans the sample times each frame, then draws each sample over the static background already painted, restores only the touched pixels between samples, and averages them in an integer accumulator that grows to what the samples touched); `layout/split_wrap.rs` places a `data-hf-split-wrap` element at rest and scale 1 (`Renderer::rest_rect`, sharing `draw::place` and `draw::content_box` with drawing) and measures its words with the flex sizing used for drawing, between the two parses; `allocation.rs` checks frame/layer byte sizes and coordinate conversions; `geometry.rs` and `frame_state.rs` hold small shared types.
- `src/captions/`: transcript domain, independent of HTML and rendering. `transcript.rs` imports `transcript.json`, OpenAI Whisper and whisper.cpp JSON, SRT, and VTT into timed words; `cues.rs` groups words into cues; `export.rs` writes SRT, VTT, and the word array. It never transcribes audio.
- `src/video/`: `<video>` sources. `colour.rs` reads SPS colour tags and converts non-default pictures; `index.rs` reads the MP4 sample tables (no decoding) into display-order frame times and closed GOPs at parse time; `player.rs` decodes on demand with OpenH264, seeking to the GOP keyframe and decoding forward (closed GOPs make decoder output order equal display order); `annexb.rs` converts length-prefixed NAL units. `parse/video.rs` builds `Content::Video` (and, unless `muted`, an `AudioClip` for the source's AAC track, decoded through the same path as `<audio>`), `render/paint/video.rs` fits and blits the frame, and each renderer fork owns one player per element.
- `src/vector/`: vector path geometry. `measure.rs` flattens SVG paths (parsed through `usvg`) into a polyline with cumulative lengths and samples points and tangents by distance; `draw_on.rs` holds `DrawOnSvg`, an inline `<svg>` prepared at parse time with a placeholder in each shape's `style`, which writes the dash pattern of every stroke for one instant. `parse/vector.rs` reads `data-hf-motion-path` (into `model::MotionPath`, added to the pose in `Motion::pose`) and `data-hf-draw` (into `Content::DrawnSvg`, rasterized per frame by `render/paint/images.rs` while drawing and cached once finished).
- `src/speed.rs`: variable playback speed. `SpeedCurve` maps clip time to source time (a constant rate, or eased ramp keyframes integrated in closed form or through a per-segment cumulative table); `parse/speed.rs` reads `data-hf-speed` and `data-hf-speed-ramp`, `model::VideoSource::frame_at` picks frames through it, and `audio/varispeed.rs` reads sound at the same rate with an antialiased windowed-sinc kernel, so picture and sound share one clock.
- `src/blocks/` and `blocks/`: ready-made blocks and themes. `blocks/*.html` are `{{name|default}}` templates embedded by `catalog.rs`; `template.rs` fills them; `mod.rs` serves the `block` CLI command and the `aether_block` MCP tool.
- `src/audio/`: sound. `decode.rs` (symphonia: WAV, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, MP3 into planar `f32`), `resample.rs` (polyphase Kaiser sinc to 48 kHz), `varispeed.rs` (fractional-position reads for clips with a speed curve; clips at 1x keep the direct copy), `effects/` (per-clip effects: `automation.rs` volume curves, `eq.rs` RBJ biquad cascades and presets, `duck.rs` look-ahead sidechain envelopes and the trigger-first mixing order; `mod.rs` holds `AudioEffects` and the per-sample `Chain`), `mix.rs` (sample-accurate placement, trim, loop, fades, and volume onto one stereo timeline through each clip's effect chain, plus clipping statistics), `aac.rs` (AAC-LC through the pure-Rust `oxideav-aac`, ADTS headers stripped; the crate is vendored in `vendor/oxideav-aac` with an FFT-based forward MDCT, see its `AETHER-PATCH.md`), `mux.rs` (MP4 audio track, interleaving, and the `moov` patch that adds the edit list); `mod.rs` is the `build_track` facade.
- `src/cli/`: human-facing subcommands (`init`, `preview`, `render`, `check`, `add`, `captions`, `agent`, `doctor`). `mod.rs` is the dispatcher (legacy flag forms stay in `main.rs`), `args.rs` parses flags, `project.rs` reads `aether.json` and resolves the entry file, `variables.rs` merges `--variables`/`--variables-file` over the project's `"variables"`, `progress.rs` implements `--progress auto|json|off` on stderr for both render paths, one file per command.
- `src/setup/` and `templates/`: agent wiring used by `init` and `agent setup`. `skills.rs` embeds `skills/` into the binary and writes it to `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills` (a test fails when a skill file is missing from the table); `mcp_config.rs` detects agents and merges the `aether-motion` server into project-scoped MCP JSON. `templates/` holds the sample projects and `AGENTS.md`.
- `npm/`: npm distribution. `cli/` is the `@aether-motion/cli` launcher (Node shim that runs the platform binary), `scripts/build-packages.mjs` assembles it and the per-platform packages from release artifacts. See [releasing.md](releasing.md).
- `src/encode/`: native H.264/MP4 encoding. `mod.rs` holds the export entry points, the quality presets, and the `Muxer` that interleaves the optional AAC track; `segments.rs` is the parallel pipeline of fixed one-second segments (each with its own encoder and IDR frame) on forked renderers; `parts.rs` writes segment ranges to `.aseg` part files and assembles a set of parts into an MP4 (distributed rendering, see Encoding); `progress.rs` holds the `ProgressObserver` interface and the internal `Tracker` that counts encoded frames atomically across segment workers and forwards throttled `ProgressUpdate`s (at most one per 250 ms, always the last frame). `encode_native_with_progress` and `render_part_with_progress` take an observer; the CLI and the MCP server (`notifications/progress` for a `_meta.progressToken`) implement it. `src/profile.rs`: the opt-in `AETHER_PROFILE=1` phase timer (raster, convert, encode, and the effect-layer, bloom, and layer-blur parts of raster), summed across threads and printed on stderr by the CLI. `src/agent.rs`: structured composition checks and the agent facade; `src/agent/zones.rs`: safe-zone and caption-zone text checks, fed by `render/probe.rs` (a read-only layout probe that records text ink boxes during the normal draw pass); `src/agent/mcp.rs`: the MCP stdio protocol server and tool handlers.

## Memory and resource limits

The renderer keeps one RGBA frame, up to 64 MiB of scaled images, and up to 32 MiB of text masks. When the visible state matches the previous frame, it reuses the RGBA frame and the native encoder's YUV conversion.

Each gradient or outer-shadow tile cache retains at most 4,096 keys, including first sightings and rejected tiles. Exceeding that metadata limit returns an explicit render error; existing slots are not evicted or changed, preserving the cached/direct drawing decisions. Reduce animated size variation or split the composition when this limit is reached. Fitted-image and text-run caches also retain at most 4,096 entries; image eviction and uncached text preparation retain the same pixel calculations. Text-layout blocks already have a 4,096-key cap.

Frames and offscreen RGBA layers keep their 128 MiB per-buffer limit, with checked multiplication and widened coordinate subtraction before allocation. Rotation and element blur reject unrepresentable pixel coordinates. Shadow-edge profiles are limited to 16 MiB before allocation. These are per-resource bounds, not a guarantee of total process memory or successful allocation under memory pressure.

## Video

A `<video>` element is indexed when the composition is parsed: the container's `stts`, `ctts`, and `stss` tables give each frame's presentation time and the keyframe groups, so the clip length and the frame shown at any time are pure functions of the composition. The frame shown `local` seconds into the clip is the last source frame whose time is not after `media_start + media(local)`, where `media` is the clip's speed curve (the identity at 1x) (looped sources wrap by the source length; otherwise the last frame holds). The source frame index is part of the element's `FrameState`, so the identical-frame shortcut still applies when the source runs slower than the output.

Decoding happens at render time, per renderer: a request for frame `k` starts a fresh decoder at the keyframe of its GOP (or continues when `k` is ahead in the same GOP) and decodes forward, so sequential rendering decodes each frame once and a backward jump costs up to one GOP. Rotation metadata is applied when the picture is converted. Sources with open GOPs, B-frames (any frame shown before a frame stored ahead of it; the bundled OpenH264 2.7 decoder returns an out-of-memory state on every such stream), fragmented MP4s, mirroring, and non-H.264 codecs are rejected at parse time. The decoded picture is converted to RGBA at source size (`video/colour.rs` reads the SPS matrix and range; limited-range BT.601 uses the decoder's own conversion, everything else a fixed-point integer one), fitted to the element box with `object-fit`, and blitted opaque (no tile cache). Memory per player is the current picture; each render thread has its own players, so 4K sources cost roughly 33 MiB per picture per thread. A 1080p source costs about 23 ms per new source frame on one thread (decoding, conversion, and blit; see `BENCHMARK.md`).

## Audio

Audio is built once, on the calling thread, before the video is encoded, so the result does not depend on `--threads` (AAC encoding costs about 2.5% of real time per audio track on one core, thanks to the FFT-based MDCT in the vendored `oxideav-aac`; see `BENCHMARK.md`). The mix has two `f32` planes (8 bytes per sample frame, capped at 512 MiB, about 22 minutes); each decoded source is capped at 128 million samples (512 MiB) and is held once at 48 kHz per distinct file.

Timing is exact by construction. Times become samples with `round(seconds * 48000)`, the track spans `round(frames * 48000 / fps)` samples, and AAC frames of 1024 samples are written after the video frame during which they start. The encoder primes its first frame with 1024 zero samples; an `elst` edit list (media time 1024, duration equal to the video) hides them, and `mvhd`/`tkhd` durations equal the video's. The `mp4` crate writes no edit list, so `audio/mux.rs` rewrites the final `moov` box after the file is closed. Tests decode the MP4 audio and check a burst lands on the expected sample (see `tests/audio.rs`), and macOS `afconvert` decodes the same file to the same sample.

## Encoding

Both encoders produce H.264/yuv420p at a constant frame rate. The native encoder (OpenH264, through the `openh264` wrapper vendored in `vendor/openh264` with a `base_qp` option, see its `AETHER-PATCH.md`) runs without rate control at the fixed quantizer of the `--quality` preset (`encode::Quality`: draft 30, standard 26 (the default), high 22, max 18); `standard` is byte-identical to exports made before presets existed. `--encoder ffmpeg` uses FFmpeg from `PATH` with `veryfast` and the matching CRF (28, 25, 21, 18) (video only: compositions with `<audio>` need the native encoder). Audio is AAC-LC, 48 kHz stereo, at 192 kbit/s by default. For native exports at 1080p or higher, `--threads 2` or more overlaps drawing and color conversion with encoding through a one-frame queue.

Distributed rendering builds on the segment pipeline. `render <file> --output part.aseg --segments A..B` renders and encodes segments `A..B` (one second each; `inspect <file> --fps N` prints the count) into a part file: a small header (output size, fps, quantizer, frame and segment counts, range), the SPS/PPS, and the MP4 samples. `render <file> --output video.mp4 --parts a.aseg,b.aseg,...` checks that the parts cover every segment exactly once with the same settings and parameter sets, mixes the audio once from the composition (pass the same `--variables`), and muxes the samples with the same `Muxer` as a single render, in about a millisecond per part. Renders of one segment or less cannot be split. Separate processes or machines can therefore render ranges of one export in parallel; the API (`aether-motion-api`) shards long renders across workers this way. `render <file> --output 'frame-{frame}.png' --frames 0,45,90` writes several stills from one process.

The MP4 of assembled parts equals a single render, byte for byte, for any worker count and split. Each worker's tile caches (`render/paint/tile_cache.rs`) still decide from their own history whether a whole-pixel gradient or soft shadow is drawn from a cached tile, but an uncached draw computes exactly the texels the tile holds (gradient color x256 and alpha x4096 in box-relative coordinates; shadow coverage x65535 relative to the element), only for its visible pixels, and blends them with the same function, so the decision never changes a pixel (`tests/distributed.rs` checks 1, 2, and 4 threads and a two-part split on drifting glows and a moving shadowed card). The effect-layer `freeze`/`thaw` of the caches is kept, although with identical paths it now only affects which draws are fast.
