# Limits

- SVG (`<img src="x.svg">` or inline `<svg>`) is static: no `<text>`, SMIL animation, scripts, `<foreignObject>`, or external resources. Scripts of any kind remain unsupported by design (determinism); use the native `data-hf-*` motion instead.
- Descendants of a rotated element are clipped to its box plus its shadow reach. This is a deliberate, documented limitation.
- There is no skew, 3D, percent translation, or other transform origin; these return an explicit error.
- `filter` takes an optional `blur()`, then color functions, then an optional `drop-shadow()`, in that order (no `url()`). Blur and drop-shadow are not allowed on the root, and the root accepts no `opacity()`; children that stick out of the element by more than the blur and drop-shadow reach plus the element's shadow reach are cut. Filter amounts animate on non-root elements only; `drop-shadow()` and `opacity()` do not animate, and CSS `@keyframes` filter lists interpolate per function rather than switching discretely when their functions differ.
- `mix-blend-mode` supports the separable modes and `plus-lighter` only (no `hue`, `saturation`, `color`, or `luminosity`), never on the root, and never inside `overflow: hidden`, rotation, reveals, wipes, filters, or another blended element.
- Motion blur (`data-motion-blur`) is set for the whole composition on the root (no per-element opt-out), costs about one full draw per sample, and snapshots assume 30 fps for the shutter window.
- `backdrop-filter` supports only `blur()`, and inside `overflow: hidden` or rotated elements it blurs that element's layer, not the whole scene.
- Scene transitions cover crossfade, push, cover, wipe, whip, zoom, dips to black or white, flash, blur, glitch, and RGB split. GPU shader transitions are unsupported; the light, focus, and glitch kinds are CPU effects with fixed shapes (no custom shaders, colors, or strengths per transition). Blurring transitions fade scene edges toward what is behind, like `filter: blur`. Wipes and `data-hf-reveal` clip to the element's box, so shadows outside it are cut while the reveal is partial, and they cannot be combined with rotate.
- `data-hf-split` handles one line of plain text (up to 400 pieces) and places letters without kerning.
- `box-shadow` takes up to 8 layers, outer or `inset`; `text-shadow` takes one non-inset shadow.
- Audio: WAV, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, and MP3 sources, mono or stereo only; AAC-LC output at 48 kHz stereo (no surround, no HE-AAC). Fades are linear, loops cut hard, volume automation (`data-hf-volume-ramp`) interpolates linear gain, ducking reacts to sample peaks (no RMS or loudness detector, no compressor or limiter), the equalizer has no dynamic or linear-phase bands and its filter state starts at zero at each clip start, and `--encoder ffmpeg` cannot carry audio. 16-bit quantization has no dither. The mix is capped at about 22 minutes. `<audio>` inside a clip starts with it but is not cut when the clip ends. Variable speed (`data-hf-speed`, `data-hf-speed-ramp`) is varispeed: pitch follows the rate (no pitch-preserving time stretch), and video slow motion repeats source frames (no frame interpolation).
- Composition variables are fixed for a whole render: they cannot change over time, cannot be read by scripts (there are none), and bind only through `var(--id)`, `data-var-text`, `data-var-src`, and `data-var-if`. There is no per-instance `data-variable-values` (no nested compositions). `step`, `placeholder`, `label`, `description`, and `brandRole` are editor metadata and are not enforced. `font` values are family names resolved like `font-family` (no font `source` URLs), and `image` values are local paths (no `fit`). A number's `unit` reaches CSS only when it is `px`, `%`, `deg`, `turn`, `rad`, `grad`, `s`, or `ms`; there is no `calc()`.
